Competitive Analysis — Revised

StormLead Pro vs. the Storm-Data Market

How our pricing, features, and site messaging stack up against the companies actually competing for the same job: turning hail/storm data into roofing leads.

  Revised July 16, 2026   Public marketing sites & pricing pages
Revised after review: the first pass of this report included EagleView and RoofSnap. Neither has a hail/storm-history product — they're roof measurement and estimating tools, used downstream once you already have a lead, not competitors for finding storm-damaged properties. The industry's own "optimal storm restoration stack" is described as three separate layers: a storm-tracking layer (this category), a measurement layer (EagleView/RoofScope), and a CRM layer (AccuLynx) — so they belong in a different comparison, not this one. Swapped in the two other real storm-tracking competitors: HailStrike and Interactive Hail Maps (Hail Recon).
Pricing updated 2026-07-16: Starter moved $9.99→$14.99/mo and Pro $29.99→$39.99/mo (see docs/pricing-refresh-punchlist.md), a moderate increase informed by the gap this report found plus a margin-modeling pass that de-risked the cost side of that decision. All figures below reflect the new prices. Existing subscribers are being grandfathered at the old rates — only new signups see the new numbers.

Suggestions

What this means for our site

Put the cost-per-report math on the site, explicitly

This is our single strongest, most defensible claim and it's currently invisible: $0.53–$2/report vs. HailStrike's ~$62/report on their mid-tier plan, or HailTrace's $40+ one-off price. Right now our pricing page just lists plan features; a direct "what this costs elsewhere" comparison (even without naming competitors) would make the advantage concrete instead of requiring the visitor to do the math themselves.

Quick win

Add social proof to the landing page

All three real competitors lead with trust signals we have none of — HailTrace's "10,000+ happy clients," Interactive Hail Maps' wall of ~35 client logos, HailStrike's dedicated testimonials page. Even a placeholder trust bar (client count, reports generated, states covered) closes a credibility gap that's currently 3-for-3 against us.

Quick win

Message "no annual lock-in" against Interactive Hail Maps specifically

Their cheapest plan requires $999 upfront for one state, paid annually with no monthly option. We're the only one of the four with true month-to-month, cancel-anytime pricing plus no-expiry one-time packs. That flexibility is a real, current differentiator that isn't stated anywhere on our pricing page right now.
